Not on this list

The two blocks beside the back centre tenon. They are location pieces inside the box, carry no load, and their thickness is free — so they come out of the scrap bin rather than off a sheet. They must be 100 tall like a rib so both skins glue to them, and no thicker than 67.3, which is the clear space each side of the tenon.

Notches

Egg crate, continuous both ways, half-depth notches at every crossing. Every notch is 18 wide and 50 deep, and all the ribs of one direction are identical — so the saw is set once per direction and the whole set runs through it.

Notch depth exactly half — a set cut slightly deep sits low and loses contact with a skin along its whole length. The fit is a slip fit, not a clearance: too tight splits the rib, too loose leaves no glue line.

One direction is notched from the top edge and the other from the bottom, and whichever way round you choose, keep it the same for every rib in that direction. Positions below are from either end of the piece — the run is symmetric, so a rib fitted backwards still fits.
