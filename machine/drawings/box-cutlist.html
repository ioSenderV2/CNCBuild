Sheets

Shelf-packed with a 1.6 mm kerf and 1.6 mm off each factory edge — a Festool track saw on Baltic birch, which is all that gets removed. This is an upper bound — parts are laid in rows and never backfilled, so a real nest does better.

Notches

Egg crate, continuous both ways, half-depth notches at every crossing. Notch depth exactly half — a set cut slightly deep sits low and loses contact with a skin along its whole length. The fit is a slip fit, not a clearance: too tight splits the rib, too loose leaves no glue line.

Notch the bottom edge of every rib where it meets the bottom skin, roughly 20 × 15, so the sand can migrate between cells.
