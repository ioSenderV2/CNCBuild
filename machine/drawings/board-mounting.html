Interface board and RP23 — mounting

Outline, mounting holes and every connector read from interface-board.kicad_pcb; the connector rectangles are the parts' real courtyards. RP23 figures his, 2026-11-08. Drawn to scale. All coordinates are millimetres from each board's own top-left corner. Regenerate with tools/make-board-sheet.py after any board change.

The interface board's four ø3.2 holes are NOT a rectangle — drill from these four numbers, never by symmetry: MH1 22.0, 4.0, MH2 94.0, 4.0, MH3 81.0, 41.0, MH4 92.0, 99.8. The RP23's four M3 ARE a true 34 square, 2 mm in from each edge of its 38.

Every interface board hole is about 4 mm from an edge and the closest is 4.0. A standoff whose flange is wider than roughly 8 mm will overhang there. Check the standoff against this before drilling the plate, not after.
