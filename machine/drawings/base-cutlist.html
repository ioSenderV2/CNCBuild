Base — cutlist and sheet layout

56 pieces, 3 sheets of 18 mm Baltic birch. Millimetres throughout. Grouped the way the base goes together, so a group is one assembly and carries everything that goes into it.

Leg strips are ripped at 90 and trimmed to 86 once the lamination is dry, so every piece cut from strip stock — fillers and foot spacers included — is listed at 90 wide.

The glue-up spacers are not in this list. One set holds a leg's slot open while it is clamped and comes straight back out to be used on the next leg, so it is one set cut from rail offcut, waxed or taped, not a part of the base.

Kerf 1.6, edge trim 1.6 — one pass off a factory edge and nothing more, so a usable 4 × 8 is 1216 × 2435.2.

How it divides across the sheets

Each sheet is loaded from one end, longest pieces first, so what is left over is one whole offcut rather than a fringe of strips. A band is as deep as the longest piece in it and gets filled across the sheet's width.

🔴 The rear shear wall takes a band of its own with nothing beside it. Its 1216 length IS the sheet's usable width, so there is no room for a neighbour and its two ends are the trim cuts.
